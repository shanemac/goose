Goose

Anyone can now build almost anything, alone, in private, talking to an AI.

That's new, and it's a trap. It fails you in two ways.

You get lonely. You talk to agents all day and stop talking to people. The agent always agrees with you. It never churns, never pays, and never tells you the thing is confusing.

You overbuild. When building is nearly free, you build everything, long before you learn whether anyone wants any of it.

So here's the whole idea. When you can build everything, the only thing that matters is not building the wrong thing. And the only way to know is to put it in front of real people and listen.

Goose is a co-founder for that. It's a Grok Bot whose core job is to keep pushing you out of the chat and toward people. Ship to production. Talk to users. Count the conversations. Cut features. Find where there's real pull.

And it does everything else you don't want to: the setup, the repo, the feedback triage, the docs, the outreach drafts. A good partner says "I'll just do it." So does Goose.

  • Every weekday morning, it asks what you're shipping today and who will see it.
  • Every Friday, it asks what shipped, how many users you talked to, where the pull is, and what to cut.
  • Every month, it checks whether you're default alive and whether this is still the mission.
  • It owns your mission, values, and strategy, so you never have to sit down and write them.

It's named for the geese that fly in a V. They take turns at the front, and the ones behind honk to keep the leader going.

Goose is an agent too, so it's built to never become your substitute for real people. Every conversation with it ends pointed at a human.
